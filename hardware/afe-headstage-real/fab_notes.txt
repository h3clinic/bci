----------------------
  Layers:                4 (F.Cu / In1.Cu / In2.Cu / B.Cu)
  Finished thickness:    1.6 mm ±10%
  Copper weight outer:   1 oz (35 µm) — F.Cu and B.Cu
  Copper weight inner:   1 oz (35 µm) — In1.Cu and In2.Cu
  Dielectric:            FR4, Tg ≥ 150 °C
  Board dimensions:      30.0 × 24.0 mm
  Surface finish:        ENIG
  Soldermask:            Both sides, green
  Silkscreen:            Front side only, white

  No material substitutions without written approval.

2. DESIGN RULES
---------------
  Minimum trace width:   0.15 mm
  Minimum clearance:     0.20 mm
  Minimum finished PTH drill: 0.20 mm
  Maximum via aspect ratio:   8:1
  All plated holes are through-hole. No blind or buried vias.

3. SOLDERMASK & VIA TENTING
----------------------------
  All non-VIP vias shall be soldermask tented both sides.

  CLAUSE SM-1: DO NOT MODIFY SOLDERMASK EXPANSION RULES.
  Mask openings over J5 pads shall remain as provided in Gerbers.
  Do not auto-adjust or override mask openings on any pad or via.

  EXCEPTION: Via-in-pad vias at J5 — see Section 4.

  CLAUSE SM-2: U1 THERMAL VIAS — COMPONENT-SIDE TENTING REQUIRED.
  The 9 thermal vias (0.30 mm drill) under U1 QFN-56 exposed pad sit
  inside the mask opening for the exposed pad.  These vias MUST be
  soldermask tented on the component side (F.Cu) to prevent solder
  paste from wicking into the via barrels during reflow.
  Gerber data includes tenting flags on these vias — do not remove.
  Bottom side (B.Cu) tenting is also specified in Gerbers; preserve it.
  Do not open mask over U1 thermal vias on either side.

4. VIA-IN-PAD — VIPPO (MANDATORY)
-----------------------------------
  Component:   J5 (Omnetics A79024-001, 36-pin nano-strip connector)
  Nets:        CH9, CH10, CH11, CH12, CH13, CH14, CH15, CH16,
               CH17, CH18, CH19, CH20, CH21, CH22 (14 nets)
  Via count:   14
  Via spec:    0.20 mm finished drill, 0.40 mm pad, through-hole

  CLAUSE VIP-1: VIPPO REQUIRED PER IPC-4761 TYPE VII ON J5 OMNETICS PADS.

  These 14 vias are located directly under SMD pads of J5.
  Solder mask opens over J5 pads. Vias are exposed on the component side.

  REQUIRED PROCESS: IPC-4761 Type VII (VIPPO)
    1. Epoxy plug via barrel (non-conductive)
    2. Copper cap plate over plug
    3. Planarize — final surface flush and solderable
    4. Coplanarity: ±25 µm maximum

  ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:
    - No depression or ridge detectable at 20× magnification on capped surface
    - Maximum void diameter in epoxy fill: 25 µm (verified by microsection)
    - Copper cap thickness: ≥ 15 µm
    - No resin shrinkback, delamination, or bleed beyond pad boundary

  No voiding or resin shrinkback permitted.
  Vias shall not be left unfilled, partially filled, or tented-only.

  CLAUSE SUB-1: NO MATERIAL SUBSTITUTIONS WITHOUT WRITTEN APPROVAL.
    Mask tenting, resin plug without copper cap, or partial fill is
    NOT acceptable as a substitute for IPC-4761 Type VII.
    Any process that does not include copper cap plating and
    planarization shall be rejected.

  Exact via coordinates: see vip_vias.csv (included in package).

  VENDOR ORDER FORM:
    JLC PCB:  "Via in pad plated over (VIPPO)" — Advanced Options
    PCBWay:   Via Process → "Resin Plug + Cap Plating"
    Generic:  "IPC-4761 Type VII: epoxy filled, copper capped, planarized"
