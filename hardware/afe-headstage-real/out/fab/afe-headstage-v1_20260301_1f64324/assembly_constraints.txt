1. SCOPE
--------
  This document specifies assembly constraints for the AFE Headstage v1.
  It applies to all assembly vendors and in-house assembly.
  Read fab_notes.txt first — it covers bare-board fabrication.

2. COMPONENT PLACEMENT
-----------------------
  All components are on the FRONT side (F.Cu) only.
  No bottom-side components.

  Critical components:
    U1 — RHD2132 QFN-56 (7×7 mm), center of board
    J5 — Omnetics A79024-001 (36-pin nano-strip), south of U1

3. STENCIL SPECIFICATION
-------------------------
  Stencil thickness:  0.10 mm (100 µm) — recommended for fine-pitch QFN
  Stencil material:   Laser-cut stainless steel
  Aperture shape:     Match pad geometry in F.Paste layer from Gerbers

  J5 VIP Pads:
    J5 pads have via-in-pad (VIPPO).  After VIPPO processing, pads are
    flat and solderable.  Baseline: use Gerber apertures as-is (1:1).

    PASTE VOLUME NOTE (0.635 mm pitch on ENIG + VIPPO):
    The default Gerber apertures assume planarized VIP pads and Type 4
    paste (20–38 µm).  If bridging is observed during first-article
    reflow inspection, apply a controlled aperture reduction of 5–15%
    in the X-axis (width) only — do not reduce Y-axis (length).
    This maintains adequate paste volume for wetting while reducing
    bridge risk at 0.635 mm pitch.
    If no bridging is observed at 1:1 aperture, do not reduce.

  U1 QFN Exposed Pad:
    Stencil aperture for U1 thermal pad: use windowed pattern
    (e.g., 4×4 or 5×5 sub-apertures at 50–60% coverage).
    Full-area paste on QFN thermal pad causes voiding and tombstoning.

  Do not modify Gerber paste layer apertures without written approval.

4. SOLDER PASTE
----------------
  Type:          SAC305 (Sn96.5/Ag3.0/Cu0.5), no-clean flux
  Mesh:          Type 4 (20–38 µm particle) or finer — required for
                 0.635 mm pitch at J5 and 0.50 mm QFN pitch at U1
  Shelf life:    Verify paste is within expiration date
  Storage:       Refrigerated, bring to room temperature ≥ 2 hours
                 before use

5. REFLOW PROFILE
------------------
  Profile: lead-free SAC305 per paste manufacturer datasheet.
  Typical parameters (verify with paste data sheet):

    Zone             | Temperature        | Duration
    -----------------+--------------------+---------
    Preheat          | 150–200 °C         | 60–90 s
    Soak             | 200–220 °C         | 30–60 s
    Reflow peak      | 245 ± 5 °C         | —
    Time above 217°C | —                  | 40–80 s
    Cooling rate     | < 4 °C/s           | —

  Single-sided reflow only (all components on F.Cu).
  Do not exceed 260 °C peak temperature.

  J5 CONNECTOR NOTE:
    Omnetics A79024 connector body is rated to 260 °C reflow.
    Verify with Omnetics datasheet for your specific lot.
    If hand-soldering J5: use 300 °C tip, < 3 s dwell per pad.

6. J5 CONNECTOR ASSEMBLY
--------------------------
  J5 pads are SMD (not through-hole).  Solder mask opens over pads.
  VIP vias under pads are VIPPO-filled and flush — treat as normal SMD.

  Placement:
    Use pick-and-place with vision alignment if available.
    J5 has asymmetric pin count — verify orientation before placement.
    Pin 1 indicator on silkscreen.

  Inspection after reflow:
    All 36 pads shall show visible solder fillets.
    No bridging between adjacent pads (0.635 mm pitch).
    No cold joints, no insufficient solder.

  Rework:
    If rework is needed on J5, use hot-air at 280 °C with nozzle
    sized to J5 footprint.  Do not exceed 3 rework cycles on VIP pads.

7. U1 QFN-56 ASSEMBLY
-----------------------
  U1 is QFN-56 (7×7 mm, 0.50 mm pitch, exposed thermal pad).

  Placement:
    Center pad alignment is critical.  Use vision-aligned P&P.
    Verify no rotation error — pin 1 dot on silkscreen.

  Thermal pad:
    9 thermal vias (0.30 mm drill) connect exposed pad to inner GND.
    Stencil uses windowed sub-apertures per Section 3.
    Thermal vias are soldermask tented on the component side (F.Cu)
    per fab_notes.txt CLAUSE SM-2.  Mask tenting prevents gross
    solder wicking through the barrel.  Minor solder migration
    into via barrels through imperfect tenting is expected and
    acceptable — this is a thermal path, not a signal path.
    If excessive voiding (>25% area) is observed at X-ray,
    verify tenting integrity before re-profiling.

  Inspection:
    X-ray recommended to verify QFN pad voiding < 25% area.
    Side fillets visible on QFN perimeter leads.

8. CLEANING
-----------
  No-clean flux: board cleaning is optional.
  If cleaning is performed: use IPA or aqueous cleaner safe for
  Omnetics connector body material (LCP).
  Do not use ultrasonic cleaning — may damage fine-pitch connectors.

9. ESD PRECAUTIONS
-------------------
  U1 (RHD2132) is ESD-sensitive (HBM Class 1C, CDM Class C3).
  J5 electrode inputs connect directly to U1 analog front-end inputs.

  Requirements:
    Assembly and handling at ESD-safe workstation (< 100 V HBM).
    Wrist straps and grounded mats mandatory.
    Store assembled boards in ESD-safe packaging.

10. MARKING & TRACEABILITY
----------------------------
  Each assembled board shall be marked with:
    - Lot number (vendor-assigned or customer-specified)
    - Date code
    - Board revision (1.0)

  If laser-marking: mark on B.Cu side silkscreen area only.
  Do not mark over component pads or solder joints.
