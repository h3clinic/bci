□ 3. NO MATERIAL SUBSTITUTIONS
    Vendor agrees to the no-substitution clause in fab_notes.txt §4:

    NOT acceptable as a substitute for IPC-4761 Type VII."

□ 4. SOLDERMASK EXPANSION LOCKED
    Vendor confirms they will NOT modify soldermask expansion values
    from the Gerber files.  Any deviation requires written approval.

□ 5. VIP VIAS CSV REVIEWED
    vip_vias.csv has been reviewed by the vendor's CAM engineer.
    All 14 VIP vias at J5 are identified and flagged for VIPPO.

□ 6. FIRST-ARTICLE MICROSECTION AGREED
    Vendor agrees to provide first-article destructive cross-section
    of at least one J5 VIP via (per fab_notes.txt §10).
    Report format: photo at 20× minimum, with via fill measurement.

□ 7. STACKUP CONFIRMED
    Vendor confirms 4-layer stackup per fab_profile.yaml:
    - 1.6 mm ±10% total thickness
    - 1 oz (35 µm) copper all layers
    - FR4 Tg ≥ 150°C
    - ENIG finish

□ 8. DRILL TABLE VERIFIED
    Vendor CAM engineer confirms drill table matches:
    - T1: 0.200 mm (28 hits) — signal vias, VIPPO flagged
    - T2: 0.300 mm (9 hits) — QFN thermal vias
    No additional drill sizes without written approval.
