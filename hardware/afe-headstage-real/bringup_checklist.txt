PURPOSE
-------
  This checklist shall be executed on the FIRST board of every fabrication
  lot before committing the lot to assembly.  Record results in writing.
  Any FAIL halts the lot.

================================================================================
 PHASE 1 — BARE BOARD VISUAL INSPECTION (before assembly)
================================================================================

Equipment: stereo microscope (≥ 20×), backlight, calipers

  [ ] 1.1  Board dimensions: 30.0 × 24.0 mm ±0.1 mm
  [ ] 1.2  4-layer stackup confirmed (view board edge cross-section or
           vendor layer count report)
  [ ] 1.3  ENIG finish: uniform gold color, no discoloration, no nickel
           bleed-through on pads
  [ ] 1.4  Soldermask: green both sides, no pinholes over copper,
           no mask on exposed pads
  [ ] 1.5  Silkscreen: legible on front, component references readable
  [ ] 1.6  Edge cuts: clean routing, no burrs, no delamination
  [ ] 1.7  Fiducials (if present): centered, clean aperture

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PHASE 2 — VIP INSPECTION AT J5 (CRITICAL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Equipment: stereo microscope (≥ 40×), coplanarity gauge or profilometer

  [ ] 2.1  All 14 VIP pads at J5 are FLAT — no visible via holes on
           component side
  [ ] 2.2  No dimples > 25 µm measured by profilometer or coplanarity gauge
  [ ] 2.3  No epoxy bleed beyond pad boundary
  [ ] 2.4  No copper cap lifting, delamination, or discoloration
  [ ] 2.5  Pad surfaces are solderable (no oxidation, no residue)
  [ ] 2.6  Compare VIP pad locations against vip_vias.csv coordinates —
           all 14 accounted for

  If ANY of 2.1–2.6 FAIL → reject lot, contact fabricator, request
  cross-section (see Phase 3).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PHASE 3 — DESTRUCTIVE CROSS-SECTION (MANDATORY for first lot)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Equipment: vendor cross-section service or in-house metallographic prep

  REQUIREMENT: One sample board from first lot SHALL be cross-sectioned
  through J5 VIP region (at least 3 of 14 VIP vias in the cut plane).
  This is destructive and mandatory. Do not skip. Do not defer.
  Assembly of remaining boards shall not proceed until cross-section
  results are reviewed and accepted.

  [ ] 3.1  Via barrel fully filled with epoxy (no voids > 25 µm)
  [ ] 3.2  Copper cap continuous and bonded to epoxy surface
  [ ] 3.3  Cap thickness ≥ 15 µm
  [ ] 3.4  No barrel cracking or delamination
  [ ] 3.5  Plating uniform on barrel walls

  Record: attach micrograph images to lot traveler.

================================================================================
 PHASE 4 — BARE BOARD ELECTRICAL TEST
================================================================================

Equipment: multimeter (DMM), or continuity tester

  [ ] 4.1  GND continuity: probe any two GND vias on opposite sides of
           board — shall read < 1 Ω
  [ ] 4.2  Power rails: probe VDD33 net at U1 pad vs nearest decoupling
           cap pad — shall read < 1 Ω
  [ ] 4.3  No shorts between adjacent electrode nets:
           For each pair (CHn, CHn+1) where n = 9..21:
             probe J5 pin for CHn and J5 pin for CHn+1
             shall read OPEN (> 10 MΩ)
  [ ] 4.4  No short between any electrode net and GND:
           For each CH9..CH22:
             probe J5 pad for CHn and any GND pad
             shall read OPEN (> 10 MΩ)
  [ ] 4.5  U1 thermal pad to GND: < 1 Ω (through thermal vias)

================================================================================
 PHASE 5 — POST-ASSEMBLY CHANNEL CONTINUITY
================================================================================

Equipment: DMM, pogo-pin test jig (recommended), or fine-pitch probes

  Precondition: U1 (RHD2132) and J5 (Omnetics A79024) are soldered.

  For each channel CH9 through CH22:

  [ ] 5.1  Continuity: J5 pin → U1 input pin
           Expected: < 5 Ω (trace + via + pad resistance)
           Record measured value per channel.

  [ ] 5.2  Isolation: each channel to GND
           Expected: > 10 MΩ

================================================================================
 PHASE 6 — CONNECTOR MECHANICAL INTEGRITY
================================================================================

Equipment: stereo microscope, tweezers

  [ ] 6.1  J5 body seated flat against board (no tilt, no lifted pads)
  [ ] 6.2  All J5 solder fillets visible under microscope (no bridging,
           no cold joints, no insufficient solder)
  [ ] 6.3  Wiggle test: apply gentle lateral force to J5 body with tweezers.
           No pad lifting. No audible cracking. No resistance change
           on channels during wiggle (monitor with DMM).
  [ ] 6.4  Mating test: insert and remove Omnetics mating connector 3×.
           Verify continuity on all 14 channels after each cycle.
  [ ] 6.5  U1 QFN inspection: no solder bridges on exposed QFN leads.
           Thermal pad: solder wetting visible at edges (X-ray if available).

================================================================================
 PHASE 7 — FUNCTIONAL SMOKE TEST
================================================================================

Equipment: bench supply (3.3V, current-limited to 100 mA), scope/logic analyzer

  [ ] 7.1  Apply 3.3 V to VDD33 rail. Current draw shall be < 20 mA
           (RHD2132 typical idle: ~8 mA). If > 50 mA → power off immediately.
  [ ] 7.2  SPI communication: send READ register command to U1.
           Verify correct device ID response.
  [ ] 7.3  Channel noise floor: configure U1 for DC-coupled input,
           read back 1000 samples per channel.
           RMS noise shall be < 5 µVrms (typical for RHD2132).
  [ ] 7.4  No latch-up: power cycle 5× with 1s on / 1s off.
           Current shall return to baseline each time.
